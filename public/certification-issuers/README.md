- Sololearn, Kangaroo Math Malaysia, Taekwondo Malaysia, IIIT, and the Royal Commonwealth Society: assets served by the issuers' own websites.
- Bursa Malaysia: current standard logo from Wikimedia Commons, based on the official logo. [Source and licence](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Bursa_Malaysia_(MSX)_logo.svg).
- SMK Sultan Abdul Samad, **Petaling Jaya**: crest from the [school's KRS site](https://krssmksaspj.wixsite.com/kadetremajasekolah). The certificate issuer's LinkedIn page identifies `smksaspj.edu.my` as its website. The crest reads “Sekolah Sultan Abdul Samad Petaling Jaya” and “Belajar Berbakti”; it is distinct from the school in Klang.

Logos identify the certificate issuers; their respective owners retain their trademarks. The relevance order is maintained in `models/certificationModel.ts`, with web development and Python programming first. Future unlisted certificates retain their database date order after the prioritised entries and use the award icon if their issuer has no mapped logo.
